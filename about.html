Our Story

From a single kitchen to a citywide craving — this is how Bite Box came to be.

Since 2016

Born From a Craving for Better Fast Food

Bite Box started as a tiny 200 sq. ft. kitchen with one mission: prove that fast food didn't have to mean low quality. No shortcuts, no frozen fillers — just honest ingredients cooked with intention.

A decade later, that same obsession with flavor and freshness powers every box we send out — plated with the same care as the very first order.
